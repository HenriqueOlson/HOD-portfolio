## Sales Prototypes

### Context

Sales representatives used prototypes to show prospective clients the platform's capabilities. They often asked designers to adjust prototypes to match a client's website and brand. This work usually took a couple of days, but short deadlines led to rushed prototypes with missing links and gaps that interfered with sales.

## Storybook, consistency for future

<h3 id="reimagining-the-architecture-and-adding-a-layer-of-control-able-to-scale-for-new-lines-of-business" class="case-study-deck">Reimagining the architecture and adding a layer of control, able to scale for new lines of business</h3>

#### Background

Moving forward from the prototype solution, a challenge became clear: how could we control what AI generated and ensure consistency for current and new features, now that teams could explore features more easily? The platform's original information architecture had lost hierarchy over time as new functions and actions were added. Its design system was not meeting the needs of this range of products.

#### Vision

My goal was to start a scalable strategy that would change how Kore builds its products. AI could power the work, but research was needed to establish the knowledge base and direction for the design system.

##### Current patterns

<ul class="case-insight-list">
  <li>The highest level was a choice between personal or company-based features.</li>
  <li>Some users could own multiple clients under company-based features.</li>
  <li>The loaded content level lacked hierarchy.</li>
</ul>

##### Current processes

<ul class="case-insight-list">
  <li>Different codebases were used for white-label client platforms and the Kore platform, increasing the time, cost, and effort needed to keep them consistent and up to date.</li>
</ul>

##### Uncover opportunities

<ul class="case-insight-list">
  <li>Assess where new scalable components could be included.</li>
</ul>

#### Solution

I started executing my vision by evolving the design system into a code based components storybook. It guided exploration and supported consistent AI generation through a configured Storybook skill. The standards established for control and consistency were:

<ol class="case-standard-list">
  <li><span class="case-standard-number" aria-hidden="true">01</span>Components built on a token architecture to control white-label customization intentionally.</li>
  <li><span class="case-standard-number" aria-hidden="true">02</span>A remodeled hierarchy to support new lines of business through a component named Shell.</li>
  <li><span class="case-standard-number" aria-hidden="true">03</span>Hybrid components that cover multiple scenarios.</li>
  <li><span class="case-standard-number" aria-hidden="true">04</span>Adaptive design readiness.</li>
</ol>

## DealRoom, the debt

<p class="case-study-deck">Allow companies to start the offering setup themselves, reducing back-and-forth with Customer Service and Engineering.</p>

### Project Overview

KoreInside is an ecosystem for private markets, a compliance-first environment for multiple client profiles. DealRoom is where a new offering comes to life through the issuance process.

### Debt

When a company wants to raise capital, the information gathering and offering setup are handled manually by an internal engineer and a Client Success agent. This can take several days or weeks, with many back-and-forth emails.

### Goal

Enable companies to configure the offering themselves.

### AI-first approach

With Storybook available, it became faster to build, review, and refine flows internally through workshop recordings and AI skills, while ensuring consistency.

### Design challenge

Guide companies through all regulatory information required to start an offering. The volume of information, often uploaded by different agents working in the same environment, makes DealRoom challenging.

### Solution

Centralize the most crucial forms in the full Wizard component for a focused experience.
